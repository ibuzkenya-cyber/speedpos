IBUZPOS SYSTEM — Policies & User Rights

Privacy, data protection, system use and refund policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Important: This is the policy template supplied for IBUZPOS SYSTEM. Review it against your actual system features, storage arrangements, contact details and purchase terms before publishing.

1. Introduction

IBUZPOS System is a business management and Point of Sale (POS) solution designed to help businesses manage daily operations efficiently. It supports recording and tracking sales, stock levels, purchases, expenses, payments and business transactions. By using the system, users agree to use it responsibly and in accordance with this policy.

2. Purpose of the System

  • Record and monitor daily sales and transactions.
  • Manage opening stock, purchases, sold stock and closing stock.
  • Track buying prices, selling prices and estimated profits.
  • Record customer payments and outstanding balances.
  • Monitor expenses and cash movements.
  • Generate daily, weekly and monthly reports.
  • Manage products, services, inventory and stock movements.
  • Print or save receipts and transaction records.

Business owners remain responsible for verifying records, financial calculations, tax obligations and business decisions.

3. Information the System May Store

Depending on the features used, the system may store business contact details, usernames and permissions, product and inventory records, buying and selling prices, sales and payment records, customer details and balances, supplier details, purchases, expenses, staff activity, reports and backups. Only enter personal information necessary for legitimate business purposes.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to record transactions, calculate sales and stock balances, generate receipts and reports, track payments and expenses, identify discrepancies, support business planning, maintain transaction history and help resolve disputes or improve system security and reliability. Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. Privacy and Data Protection

IBUZPOS SYSTEM aims to handle personal and business information responsibly and in line with applicable Kenyan data protection laws, including the Data Protection Act, 2019. Users are responsible for the accuracy of information they enter and for having the necessary authority or consent to record other people's personal information. Confidential customer, employee and business information must not be shared with unauthorised persons.

6. User Rights and Responsibilities

  • Access control: Users should be given access only to functions and information needed for their duties.
  • Confidentiality: Keep passwords and login details private and log out of shared computers.
  • Accuracy: Check sales, stock, payments, balances and reports regularly.
  • Data care: Create regular backups and keep them in a secure location.
  • Responsible use: Do not access, change, disclose or delete business information without authorisation.

Where supported, administrator accounts may manage staff accounts and access permissions. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Storage, Retention and Backups

Storage depends on the version and hosting arrangement. Offline installations may store records on the local computer or device; hosted installations may store information on hosting infrastructure. Back up records before changing computers, reinstalling or updating the system. Retain records only as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, accounting, dispute resolution and legal compliance, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

The system may be used with third-party hosting providers, payment services, messaging platforms, printers or other business tools. Those services may have their own terms and privacy policies. IBUZPOS SYSTEM is not a bank, payment processor, government tax authority or official accounting regulator unless a specific service has been separately authorised and documented.

9. Accuracy of Transactions and Stock

Report accuracy depends on the information entered and correct system use. Regularly verify opening and closing stock, purchases, sales, returns, damaged goods, payment records, customer balances, expenses and prices. Compare system reports with physical stock counts, payment statements and supporting records. Estimated profit may not equal final net profit after expenses, taxes and adjustments.

10. Refund Policy

Refunds for subscriptions, licences, installation, customisation or other paid services are subject to purchase terms communicated to the customer. Confirm the package, price, features, installation requirements and payment details before purchasing.

Refund request deadline: Unless different terms are agreed in writing or applicable law requires otherwise, customers should submit requests within 24 hours of payment. This deadline does not override any right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Processing: Where approved, IBUZPOS SYSTEM will aim to process refunds within 1 to 24 hours after receiving and verifying the necessary information. Actual receipt may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

Possible grounds for consideration: duplicate charges; payment received but the agreed service or licence was not supplied; a provider-attributable delivery or technical problem that prevents delivery and cannot reasonably be resolved; or a refund required by law or written agreement.

Requests may need review if the wrong package was selected, the customer changed their mind after installation or activation, the system is incompatible with the device, the issue results from user error, unauthorised changes, damaged equipment or third-party services, or custom development or installation has already been delivered. These circumstances do not automatically determine the outcome; the agreement, circumstances and applicable law will be considered.

11. Information for Refund Requests

Provide the purchaser's name, payment telephone number, transaction code or receipt, product/licence/service purchased, reason for the request and reasonable supporting information. Never provide a mobile money PIN, banking password or other secret authentication credentials.

12. Availability and Limitations

Availability depends on the installed version, device, configuration, power, storage and connected services. Offline installations may support some functions without internet, while online integrations may require connectivity. Do not rely on the system as the only record of important transactions; maintain backups and verify records independently.

13. Changes to this Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in features, security practices, business arrangements or legal requirements. The latest version should be made available through the relevant website, application or official communication channel.

14. Contact and Support

For privacy, stock management, transaction records, technical support or refund questions, contact the official IBUZPOS SYSTEM support team using the contact details published by the system provider.
